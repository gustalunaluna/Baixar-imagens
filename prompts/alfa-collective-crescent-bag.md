# Prompt pronto — bolsa meia-lua Alfa Collective

Construído sobre a estrutura de nove blocos de `produto-para-video.md`.
Entrada: **1 foto do produto** (frente). As outras duas fotos servem de apoio
para escrever o bloco 02 — não precisam ser enviadas ao modelo.

**A ideia do filme:** bolsa toda preta, fosca, discreta — que abre e revela um
forro azul cobalto. O preto-no-preto é a paciência; o azul é o pagamento. Toda
a montagem existe para chegar nesse corte.

---

## 01 / CREATIVE DIRECTION

Create a photorealistic product film from the supplied product reference.
Follow the journey from raw black crinkle nylon to a finished crescent
crossbody bag, ending with the bag worn on the street. The visual language is
tactile, restrained and cinematic: honest workshop craftsmanship, matte
technical fabric, tonal black-on-black branding and one deliberate flash of
cobalt blue lining. The film is almost monochrome until the bag opens. Every
shot must feel physically photographed.

## 02 / PRODUCT IDENTITY

Match the reference bag exactly.

Preserve its crescent half-moon silhouette: a wide, gently curved top edge and
a deep rounded base, soft and unstructured, with no rigid frame. The body is
matte black crinkled technical nylon with fine irregular wrinkles that catch
light softly and never look glossy or plastic.

A coil zipper runs along the entire curved top opening, with black tape and a
dark antiqued metal pull shaped like the "A" monogram.

On the front, a rounded-rectangle patch pocket is applied to the centre, with
its own curved zipper on the left side and a small matching pull, plus a small
dark woven tag at the base of that zipper. On the upper area of this pocket,
"Alfa Collective®" is embroidered in small white serif letters. Below it sits a
large tonal logo — a serif "A" crossed by three slanting strokes — rendered in
satin black on the matte black surface, readable only by the difference in
sheen.

On the back, a curved seam crosses the lower third, and a blue oval woven patch
with the silver "A" monogram sits centred just below it. This patch is the only
colour on the exterior.

A flat slip pocket sits at each end of the bag.

The strap is wide flat black webbing, adjustable through a matte dark slider,
long enough to be worn crossbody.

The interior lining is cobalt blue.

Preserve the pattern scale, proportions, seam placement, stitching and hardware
count across every cut.

## 03 / MATERIALS

The crinkled nylon keeps its wrinkle structure at every scale: the creases are
in the cloth, not painted on it, and they move with the fabric rather than
sliding across it. The surface is matte with a soft, diffuse sheen and never
reads as leather, vinyl or plastic.

The tonal front logo is a satin finish sitting flush on the fabric, separated
from the body only by sheen — never a raised sticker and never a lighter grey.
White embroidery shows individual stitches and slight thread relief.

The blue oval patch has a woven surface with a clean stitched border.

Metal hardware is dark, matte and softly worn, with narrow reflected highlights
and believable micro scratches.

The cobalt lining is smooth, lighter in weight than the shell, and clearly a
different material.

Webbing shows visible weave and stiff, springy behaviour — it does not drape
like silk.

Keep textile texture stable as focus and camera position change.

## 04 / SHOT SEQUENCE

OPEN on an extreme macro of a roll of black crinkle nylon unfolding, the
wrinkles releasing under a hand. CUT to a rotary cutter travelling along a
marked crescent curve, revealing a clean fabric edge. MOVE to a sewing machine
needle piercing the black nylon, the presser foot advancing the fabric in
small, physically accurate steps. CUT to an embroidery head laying the white
"Alfa Collective®" lettering stitch by stitch. MOVE into a shallow-focus glide
across the tonal "A" logo, where the light rolls over it and the shape appears
out of the black. CUT to the zipper being closed, the dark metal "A" pull
travelling along the curved top edge. REVEAL the cobalt blue lining as the bag
opens — the first colour in the film. CUT to the blue oval patch on the back,
sharp against matte black. PULL BACK to the complete bag resting on a pale
neutral surface. FINISH with the bag worn crossbody by a person walking, the
body settling against the hip, a brief flash of blue as the top shifts open.

## 05 / CAMERA & LENSES

Use a 100 mm macro look for fabric weave, needle, stitching, embroidery and
hardware. Shift to an 85 mm product lens for the complete silhouette, then a
50 mm editorial lens for the final walking shot. Motion is controlled: slow
lateral slides, short push-ins and deliberate focus pulls. Cut on hand movement
and on the travel of the zipper pull. Maintain natural perspective and
realistic depth of field. Avoid exaggerated lens distortion or unstable camera
drift.

## 06 / LIGHTING & COLOUR

Shape the workshop scenes with soft directional window light and deep, clean
shadows. Black on black must stay readable: use grazing light across the
surface so wrinkle, seam and tonal logo separate by texture rather than by
brightness. Never lift the blacks into grey and never crush the shadow detail.
Add narrow specular highlights to the dark hardware without clipping. The
product reveal uses a pale neutral surface against a subdued background. The
cobalt lining stays saturated and true, never turning purple or cyan. Outdoors,
overcast daylight keeps the black matte and the blue honest. Skin tones stay
natural.

## 07 / MOTION & CONTINUITY

Hands apply credible pressure to the fabric. The needle reciprocates vertically
while the material feeds forward. The rotary cutter rolls rather than drags.
The nylon crumples and recovers with the stiffness of technical fabric, not
like cotton and not like rubber. The zipper pull travels along the curve with
the teeth engaging progressively behind it. The unstructured body sags under
its own weight and reshapes when touched. The webbing strap carries the weight
without stretching or changing length, and the slider stays where it was set.
During the walking shot, the bag swings slightly out of phase with the wearer
and settles back against the body. Maintain consistent product identity,
material scale, seam placement and attachment points throughout.

## 08 / EDIT & SOUND

Build a concise, patient rhythm: raw material, cutting, stitching, embroidery,
the tonal logo, the zipper, then the colour reveal, then the street. Use clean
cuts and motivated transitions. Let sewing-machine clicks, the dry rustle of
crinkle nylon, the cutter rolling and a single crisp zipper run support the
imagery. Hold silence before the lining reveal. Keep the final product readable
before the film ends. Deliver crisp, natural motion and a refined campaign
finish.

## 09 / QUALITY CONSTRAINTS

No altered logo, extra pockets, missing zipper pull, floating hardware or
distorted crescent silhouette. Do not change the tonal logo into a light grey
or white one, do not move the blue oval patch to the front, and do not add
colour to the exterior beyond that patch. No melting fabric, plastic-looking
nylon, smooth wrinkle-free body, impossible reflections, warped hands or
inconsistent stitching. The lining stays cobalt blue in every frame it appears.
Do not add titles, captions or new branding inside the generated footage.
Preserve the supplied product design in every frame.

## NOTAS

- **O bloco 06 é o mais importante deste prompt específico.** Produto todo preto
  e fosco é o caso em que IA mais erra: ou lava o preto para cinza, ou entope a
  sombra e some com a costura. A instrução `use grazing light across the surface
  so wrinkle, seam and tonal logo separate by texture rather than by brightness`
  é o que resolve — a luz raspando é como se fotografa preto de verdade.
- **O logo tonal é a armadilha.** A IA tende a "ajudar" clareando o logo para
  ficar legível. Por isso ele aparece duas vezes: no 03 (`separated from the
  body only by sheen`) e no 09 (`do not change the tonal logo into a light grey
  or white one`).
- **O forro azul é o roteiro.** Segurar a cor até a abertura, com silêncio antes
  do corte, é o que transforma uma sequência de fábrica em filme.
- Este prompt descreve um produto da **Alfa Collective**. Para publicar como
  peça nossa, o produto e a marca têm que ser os nossos — a estrutura é o que
  se reaproveita.
