# Prompt de FOTO — bolsa meia-lua, doca de carga noturna

Referência visual: foto de streetwear em doca de carga — baú de caminhão
aberto, caixas de papelão com o logo da marca, grande angular fisheye, luz
dura, sombra esmagada, listras refletivas laranja e lanterna do caminhão como
acentos. Estética de **dia de despacho**: a marca saindo para a rua.

Produto: a bolsa meia-lua preta da Alfa Collective.

---

## A ADAPTAÇÃO QUE PRECISOU SER FEITA

Nessa referência a sombra vai até o breu — é isso que dá o clima. Só que uma
bolsa **preta fosca** jogada numa cena dessas **some**: vira um buraco no meio
do quadro.

A saída não é clarear a cena, que mataria a estética. É de composição:
**a bolsa fica contra o papelão**, que é a única massa clara e quente da foto,
e a luz do baú desenha a borda dela por trás. Preto sobre kraft separa; preto
sobre sombra, não.

---

## O PROMPT

**01 / IMAGE BRIEF**
> A single photorealistic photograph for a streetwear brand campaign, shot like
> documentary coverage of a shipment leaving the warehouse on drop day. Gritty,
> dark, high contrast, physically photographed on a real loading dock at night —
> never rendered, never studio-clean. The supplied bag is worn by the subject
> and must remain the clearest, most legible object in the frame.

**02 / PRODUCT IDENTITY**
> Match the reference bag exactly. Preserve its crescent half-moon silhouette:
> a wide, gently curved top edge and a deep rounded base, soft and
> unstructured, with no rigid frame. The body is matte black crinkled technical
> nylon with fine irregular wrinkles. A coil zipper runs along the entire curved
> top opening, with black tape and a dark antiqued metal pull shaped like the
> "A" monogram. On the front, a rounded-rectangle patch pocket is applied to the
> centre, with its own curved zipper on the left side and a small matching pull,
> plus a small dark woven tag at the base of that zipper. On the upper area of
> this pocket, "Alfa Collective®" is embroidered in small white serif letters.
> Below it sits a large tonal logo — a serif "A" crossed by three slanting
> strokes — in satin black on the matte black surface, readable only by the
> difference in sheen. A flat slip pocket sits at each end. The strap is wide
> flat black webbing with a matte dark slider, worn long across the body.
> Preserve the pattern scale, proportions, seam placement, stitching and
> hardware count.

**03 / MATERIALS**
> The crinkled nylon keeps its wrinkle structure: the creases are in the cloth,
> not painted on it, and they catch the hard light in small broken highlights.
> Matte, with a soft diffuse sheen — never glossy, never leather, never
> plastic. The tonal front logo is satin black on matte black, separated from
> the body only by sheen and by the angle of the light, never a lighter grey.
> The white embroidery is the brightest detail on the bag and stays crisp.
> Hardware is dark, matte, with narrow specular highlights. Webbing shows
> visible weave and stiff, springy behaviour.

**04 / SCENE & COMPOSITION**
> Night loading dock inside a warehouse with a ribbed corrugated metal ceiling
> arching overhead and raw concrete walls. A box truck is backed in with its
> rear shutter open, the lit interior forming a bright rectangle deep in the
> frame. Kraft cardboard boxes printed with a single large black "A" monogram
> are stacked in the right foreground and inside the truck. The reflective
> orange-and-white hazard chevrons on the truck's rear bumper catch the light.
> Red taillights glow low in the frame.
>
> A young man stands in the left foreground, seen from behind and slightly to
> the side, lifting a cardboard box up toward the truck at head height so his
> face is hidden behind it. He wears the crescent bag crossbody, the body of the
> bag resting at his hip on the camera side, fully visible and unobstructed.
>
> Frame the bag against the kraft cardboard, never against black shadow. Vertical
> 2:3 frame, heavy dark vignette, the truck opening and the box stacks leading
> the eye inward.

**05 / SUBJECT & STYLING**
> Oversized washed grey heavyweight t-shirt with a large graphic on the back,
> baggy black work trousers, worn sneakers, tattooed forearms. A second worker
> is half visible inside the truck, cropped, moving boxes. Real working posture
> and real weight in the hands — not posed, not smiling, faces not featured.
> No competing logos anywhere in the frame.

**06 / CAMERA & LENS**
> Ultra-wide 15 mm lens, close to the subject and low to the ground, tilted
> slightly up. Strong barrel distortion: the corrugated ceiling bows across the
> top of the frame, the truck edges stretch toward the corners, the foreground
> boxes loom large. Deep focus, everything from the near boxes to the truck
> interior legible. Handheld feel with a very slight imperfect horizon.

**07 / LIGHTING**
> Hard directional light spilling from inside the truck and from a single
> overhead work lamp, raking across the scene. Deep, fast falloff: the concrete
> walls and the ceiling corners fall to near black. No fill, no softbox, no
> even studio light.
>
> The bag is lit deliberately: a narrow rim of light from the truck defines its
> crescent edge and the top of the strap, while a spill of bounced light off the
> kraft cardboard opens the front pocket just enough to read the embroidery, the
> tonal logo and the wrinkle of the nylon. The bag is never a flat black hole
> and never fully swallowed by shadow.
>
> Practical accents: red taillights, the reflective hazard chevrons glowing,
> and a cool strip of light inside the truck.

**08 / COLOUR**
> Desaturated overall with cool teal-green shadows and a warm kraft cardboard
> mid-tone as the only large warm mass. Orange safety-stripe and red taillight
> accents. Blacks are deep and slightly cool but retain some detail where the
> bag is. Visible high-ISO grain and a little halation on the bright highlights.
> No orange-and-teal grade pushed to cliché, no film-emulation filter, no
> artificial bloom.

**09 / QUALITY CONSTRAINTS**
> No altered logo, extra pockets, missing zipper pull, floating hardware or
> distorted crescent silhouette. Do not change the tonal logo into a light grey
> or white one. Do not add colour to the bag's exterior. The bag must not be
> cropped, occluded by an arm, or lost in shadow. No plastic-looking nylon, no
> wrinkle-free body, no warped hands or extra fingers, no impossible
> reflections, no floating boxes. The only branding anywhere in the frame is the
> "A" monogram on the boxes and on the bag — no other logos, no titles, no
> captions, no watermarks, no invented text on the cardboard. Preserve the
> supplied product design exactly.

Para uma foto mais de produto e menos de cena, troque o bloco 04 por:

> The bag rests on top of a stack of kraft cardboard boxes printed with the "A"
> monogram, in the foreground of the same night loading dock, the open truck
> glowing out of focus behind it. The strap falls loose over the edge of the

> bag large in frame and the dock stretching away behind.

O forro azul contra o papelão e o breu é o quadro mais forte que essa cena
permite. Guarde para a foto de fechamento.

## NOTAS

- **A regra que faz esta foto funcionar: preto sobre kraft, nunca preto sobre
  sombra.** É a única forma de manter a estética esmagada da referência e ainda
  enxergar a bolsa. Está escrita duas vezes de propósito (04 e 07).
- A referência esconde o rosto atrás da caixa. Mantive: tira a pessoa da
  disputa e deixa o produto e a logística serem o assunto. É o mesmo mecanismo
  da foto da Pas Normal, com outro figurino.
- **`the only branding anywhere in the frame is the "A" monogram`** é
  obrigatório aqui. Cena de galpão com caixa de papelão é onde a IA mais
  inventa texto: escreve palavra tortas na caixa, cria marca de transportadora,
  põe etiqueta ilegível. O bloco 09 fecha isso.
- Grão alto e halação são parte da estética, não defeito — sem eles a foto fica
  com cara de render limpo e perde o registro documental.
