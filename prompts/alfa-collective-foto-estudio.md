# Prompt de FOTO — bolsa meia-lua Alfa Collective, estúdio editorial

Referência visual: campanha da **Pas Normal Studios** (ciclorama claro, luz
frontal ampla, banquinho de pernas cromadas, produto levantado cobrindo o
rosto). A referência é usada para **luz, cenário, enquadramento e styling** —
o produto é a bolsa meia-lua preta.

Entrada: a foto do produto. Saída: still vertical.

---

## A ADAPTAÇÃO QUE PRECISOU SER FEITA

Na referência o produto é **verde oliva sobre cinza claro**: separa sozinho.
A nossa bolsa é **preta fosca**. Com a luz frontal e macia da referência —
e só ela — a bolsa vira recorte preto chapado: some a ruga do nylon, some a
costura, some o logo tonal.

Então: cenário e luz principal iguais à referência, mais **duas strip lights
raspando por trás em contraluz lateral**, que desenham a borda da meia-lua e
trazem a textura de volta sem sujar o fundo. É a única mudança estrutural.

## O PROMPT — FOTO COM PESSOA (principal)

**01 / IMAGE BRIEF**
> A single photorealistic studio photograph for a technical-apparel brand
> campaign. Clinical, deadpan, Scandinavian sportswear register. The supplied
> bag is the subject: it is held up in front of the model's face so the product,
> not the person, is what the frame is about. Shot on a seamless studio
> backdrop. The image must look physically photographed on a real set, not
> rendered or composited.

**02 / PRODUCT IDENTITY**
> Match the reference bag exactly. Preserve its crescent half-moon silhouette:
> a wide, gently curved top edge and a deep rounded base, soft and
> unstructured, with no rigid frame. The body is matte black crinkled technical
> nylon with fine irregular wrinkles. A coil zipper runs along the entire curved
> top opening, with black tape and a dark antiqued metal pull shaped like the
> "A" monogram. On the front, a rounded-rectangle patch pocket is applied to the
> centre, with its own curved zipper on the left side and a small matching pull,
> plus a small dark woven tag at the base of that zipper. On the upper area of
> this pocket, "Alfa Collective®" is embroidered in small white serif letters.
> Below it sits a large tonal logo — a serif "A" crossed by three slanting
> strokes — in satin black on the matte black surface, readable only by the
> difference in sheen. A flat slip pocket sits at each end of the bag. The strap
> is wide flat black webbing, adjustable through a matte dark slider. Preserve
> the pattern scale, proportions, seam placement, stitching and hardware count.

**03 / MATERIALS**
> The crinkled nylon keeps its wrinkle structure: the creases are in the cloth,
> not painted on it. The surface is matte with a soft, diffuse sheen and never
> reads as leather, vinyl or plastic. The tonal front logo is a satin finish
> flush on the fabric, separated from the body only by sheen — never a lighter
> grey and never a raised sticker. White embroidery shows individual stitches
> and slight thread relief. Hardware is dark, matte, with narrow highlights.
> Webbing shows visible weave and stiff, springy behaviour.

**04 / SCENE & COMPOSITION**
> Seamless pale cool-grey studio backdrop, edge to edge, with no visible horizon
> line, no props, no set dressing. A model sits on a low stool with a black seat
> and slim polished chrome tubular legs, knees apart, feet planted. The model
> holds the bag up by its webbing strap with both hands raised above, so the
> crescent body hangs vertically and covers the face completely. Vertical 4:5
> frame. Full body, cropped just below the feet. The model is centred; the bag
> sits in the upper third, at the optical centre of the frame. Generous empty
> space above and to the sides. Symmetrical, frontal, deadpan.

**05 / SUBJECT & STYLING**
> Monochrome technical sportswear so nothing competes with the product: black
> fitted technical shorts, white ribbed crew socks, a white long-sleeve base
> layer with the cuffs showing, and a black nylon shell worn loose over it. No
> visible competing logos, no jewellery, no colour. Natural, unstyled skin.
> Relaxed, neutral posture — no performed expression, and the face is hidden by
> the bag anyway.

**06 / CAMERA & LENS**
> 105 mm lens on a tripod, camera level with the raised bag, no tilt, straight-on
> perspective. Aperture around f/8: the whole bag is sharp front to back, the
> backdrop stays clean. Natural perspective, no wide-angle distortion. The
> product is rendered at high resolution with visible fabric grain.

**07 / LIGHTING**
> A very large diffused frontal key, slightly above and centred, giving soft,
> almost shadowless illumination with a short, soft shadow directly beneath the
> stool. Broad fill keeps the backdrop clean and evenly lit, with only a faint
> falloff toward the corners.
>
> Because the bag is matte black on a pale backdrop, add two narrow strip lights
> at roughly 45 degrees behind and to each side, grazing across the surface.
> They must define the crescent edge, the wrinkle structure of the nylon, the
> seams and the tonal logo through texture rather than brightness. Keep these
> highlights narrow and controlled — no blown-out hot spots, no glossy sheen,
> no spill onto the backdrop.
>
> The black must stay black: never lifted into grey, never crushed into a flat
> silhouette. Every seam and every crease stays legible.

**08 / COLOUR**
> Cool neutral white balance, low saturation overall. The backdrop is a pale
> cool grey. Blacks are neutral, not blue and not brown. Skin tones stay
> natural and slightly warm against the cool set. No colour grading effect,
> no film emulation, no vignette.

**09 / QUALITY CONSTRAINTS**
> No altered logo, extra pockets, missing zipper pull, floating hardware or
> distorted crescent silhouette. Do not change the tonal logo into a light grey
> or white one. Do not add colour to the exterior. No plastic-looking nylon, no
> smooth wrinkle-free body, no oversmoothed skin, no warped hands or extra
> fingers, no inconsistent stitching, no impossible reflections. Do not add
> titles, captions, watermarks or new branding. Preserve the supplied product
> design exactly.

## NOTAS

- **O bloco 07 é o que vale.** Copiar só "luz suave e frontal" da referência
  entrega uma mancha preta. As duas strip lights raspando são o que faz um
  produto preto fosco existir numa foto clara.
- **`readable only by the difference in sheen`** e **`do not change the tonal
  logo into a light grey or white one`** precisam aparecer nos dois lugares
  (03 e 09). A IA insiste em clarear o logo para "ajudar".
- O truque de composição da referência — produto cobrindo o rosto — funciona
  porque tira a pessoa da disputa. Com tote é natural (duas alças); com bolsa
  de alça única, segurar a alça no alto e deixar a meia-lua pendurar dá o mesmo
  efeito.
- A referência é da Pas Normal Studios e o produto é da Alfa Collective. O que
  se reaproveita é a linguagem de luz e cenário — o produto e a marca da foto
  publicada têm que ser os nossos.
